POLICY / 2026-08-27

Privacy, kept local.

Agent Write Barrier has no telemetry, account, analytics, cookies, or network calls. The CLI reads only the policy and filesystem roots you select.

Receipts

Write receipts stay on your device. They contain local paths, file metadata, hashes, and the wrapped command. Review them before sharing because paths may reveal project or user names.

Documentation site

This site loads no third-party scripts, fonts, or trackers. Its service worker caches public site files on your device for offline reading. Clear the site’s storage in your browser to remove that cache.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project repository.
